# A New Challenge: OPCODE IMPACT 2026

I am incredibly happy to announce that I've had the opportunity to work alongside **Jyothi Engineering College** and my department—Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security)—in building the website for a massive upcoming project: **OPCODE IMPACT 2026**.

## What is OPCODE IMPACT?

**OPCODE IMPACT 2026** is a premier National Level 24-Hour Cybersecurity for Sustainable Development Hackathon. It is designed to bring together student engineers, researchers, and academic institutions from all across India. The core mission? To develop secure, resilient, and sustainable technological solutions that matter.

Organised by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security) at Jyothi Engineering College (Autonomous) in Thrissur, the hackathon bridges the gap between modern cybersecurity innovation and the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to address real-world challenges.
