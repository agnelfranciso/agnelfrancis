# Behind the Scenes: Organizing OPCODE Hackathon 2026

Earlier last month, I wrote about collaborating on the official website for **OPCODE IMPACT 2026**. Fast forward to this weekend, and the main event finally took place! 

Organized by our very own department—**Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security)** at Jyothi Engineering College—the hackathon brought together enthusiastic minds ready to tackle cybersecurity and real-world problem statements. 

This was my very first time experiencing a hackathon in person. Rather than just sitting on the sidelines or participating as a contestant, I had the privilege of experiencing it from the inside out: **as an organizer and volunteer**.

---

## The Overnight Hackathon Experience

One of the most thrilling parts of a 24-hour hackathon is staying overnight at the venue. There is something unique and exhilarating about walking through the halls at 2:00 AM and 4:00 AM:

- Teams deeply engrossed in their code, whiteboards filled with system architectures and logic flows.
- Sudden bursts of cheering when a pesky bug finally gets resolved or a prototype starts responding.
- Late-night coffee runs, quick conversations, and an infectious buzz of creative adrenaline filling the hall.

![The 24-hour hackathon venue at night illuminated with fairy lights and teams collaborating](/images/blog/opcode-hackathon-2026/hackathon-venue-night.jpg)

Staying overnight alongside fellow volunteers and watching the participants pour their heart and soul into building viable solutions was both inspiring and pure fun.

---

## Wearing Two Hats: Technical & Media Teams

During the event, I had the opportunity to contribute across two different verticals:

### 1. The Technical Team
On the technical side, our responsibility was ensuring things went smoothly behind the curtains. From handling system requirements, network logistics, and workspace setups to being on standby whenever participants or mentors needed assistance, it taught me how crucial rapid troubleshooting and clear coordination are in a high-stakes, time-sensitive environment.

### 2. The Media Team
On the media side, we captured the spirit of the hackathon as it unfolded. From documenting team brainstorms and stage presentations to recording candid moments of exhausted yet determined developers in the dead of night, seeing the hackathon through the lens was a blast.

Switching between technical troubleshooting and media coverage kept me on my toes throughout the 24 hours, but it was immensely fulfilling.

---

## Lessons Learned & Reflections

Looking back, being an organizer and volunteer for my first-ever hackathon was an incredible learning curve:

1. **Operations Matter:** A seamless participant experience is the result of hours of unseen background preparation, coordination, and swift issue resolution.
2. **Team Spirit & Camaraderie:** Working with our department peers and faculty through the night bonded us and showed what collective effort can accomplish.
3. **Appreciation for the Culture:** Being immersed in the hackathon atmosphere gave me huge respect for competitive programming, rapid prototyping, and the sheer grit of tech enthusiasts building under a ticking clock.

![Department organizing team, faculty, dignitaries, and participants at OPCODE IMPACT '26](/images/blog/opcode-hackathon-2026/opcode-group-photo.jpg)

A big shoutout to our department, the faculty coordinators, my fellow volunteer peers, and everyone who made **OPCODE 2026** such a memorable success. I cannot wait for the next edition!
