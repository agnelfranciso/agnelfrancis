# Selected for Smart India Hackathon!

I am incredibly happy and proud to share that my team, **SpecSync**, has been officially selected from our college to participate in the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)**! 

## What We Are Building

We are working on something really exciting and impactful: an **AI-Powered Recommendation Engine for Identifying Applicable Indian Standards for Procurement Specifications**. 

Navigating through procurement specifications and finding the exact Indian Standards that apply can be a tedious and complex process. Our solution aims to simplify and automate this using artificial intelligence, ensuring that the right standards are recommended efficiently and accurately. 

## The Journey Ahead

Being there in the beginning processes of SIH is already a huge learning experience. Representing our college is an honor, and I am looking forward to diving deep into the problem statement, refining our AI models, and building a robust solution with my amazing teammates.

Stay tuned as I'll be sharing more updates and technical deep-dives on how we are building our recommendation engine!
