*It started as a home for my own projects. It became something I couldn't build alone.*

## 01. Why I Started It

In 2025, I created FramePixel as a company just for myself. I'd been developing since 2022–2023, and I kept running into the same problem: a major project doesn't fit on a portfolio. It gets one card, one screenshot, and a link, and a serious product ends up looking like a weekend experiment.

I wanted a proper home for the bigger work, with its own name and its own domain.

---

## 02. Why Small Projects Didn't Belong

I quickly learned that not everything fits there. Small personal projects are usually unfinished, and a half-built demo under a company name isn't a showcase. It's clutter. 

So FramePixel became about one thing: **projects complete enough to be proud of.**

---

## 03. Why It Became Collaborative

Building showcase-quality projects alone is slow, and I'd already learned what working in isolation costs. When I lost years of Bussiler, no one could have caught the missing backup but me.

So I opened FramePixel up. It's now me and four students, building together on one shared domain.

---

## 04. How It Works

- **One domain, many projects.** Joint projects live in one place instead of being scattered across personal profiles.
- **Equal credit.** Everyone who contributes is credited. There's no "main account" and no sub-collaborators.
- **Portfolios stay personal.** Each of us links FramePixel projects from our own portfolio.
- **Personal projects stay personal.** Solo work lives on our own sites, not here.

---

## 05. What It Isn't (Yet)

FramePixel is a **non-registered company**. We operate like an independent studio, but we're not a legal entity, and I'd rather say that openly than let the word "company" suggest more than it is. Making it official is something we'll think about as we grow.

---

## 06. What We've Built

- **[Bussiler](/work/bussiler)** — An offline-first native Android public transit timetable and live corridor countdown app for commuters across Velur corridors.
- **[SunRoute](https://sunroute.netlify.app/)** — An algorithmic bus seat selector calculating real-time solar azimuth angles to ensure you sit in the shade on every trip.

---

## 07. Where It's Going

I don't know yet whether FramePixel stays a small group of friends or grows into something bigger. I do know the idea works: students can build real, finished things together and share the credit fairly.
